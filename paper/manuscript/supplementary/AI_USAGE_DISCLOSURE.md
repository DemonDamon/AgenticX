# AI Assistance Disclosure

In accordance with the AAMAS 2027 policy on AI-assisted technologies, we disclose the
scope of AI assistance in this work.

## 1. Code and infrastructure (AI-assisted, human-reviewed)

Experiment code — including the task generator, the 7-arm runner, assembly protocol
implementations, scoring pipeline, statistical analysis scripts, and balance/resume
guard logic — was developed with the assistance of AI coding agents (Claude/Opus-class
and GLM-5.3-class models, 2026). All code was written to human-authored specifications,
reviewed line-by-line, and executed under human supervision; all experimental decisions
(arm design, protocol definitions, statistical tests, correction thresholds) were made
by the authors.

## 2. Task content (AI-assisted drafting, human-curated)

The 15 base office tasks were drafted with AI assistance and then manually curated,
anonymized (real product and company names replaced with neutral placeholders), and
equipped with human-verified ground-truth assertions. The 150 parameterized variants
are generated by deterministic code; their ground truths are recomputed programmatically
and validated by `tasks/validate_variants.py`.

## 3. Manuscript (AI-assisted polishing)

The manuscript was drafted by the authors and polished for grammar and clarity with AI
assistance. All claims, numbers, and interpretations were written and verified by the
authors against the released run data; all cited references were manually verified
against DBLP / publisher pages.

## 4. No AI as author

No AI system is listed as an author. The authors take full responsibility for the
accuracy, originality, and integrity of this work, including the absence of plagiarized
or hallucinated content and citations.
